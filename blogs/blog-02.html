My Journey to IIT Kanpur MTech CSE

Getting into IIT Kanpur for MTech CSE was one of the most rewarding things I have done. Here is what I wish I had known at the start.

The GATE exam

GATE CS covers Data Structures, Algorithms, OS, DBMS, Networks, Theory of Computation, Discrete Math, and Linear Algebra. Depth per topic is manageable if you plan well.

I spent the first three months doing a systematic subject-by-subject pass using standard textbooks, then four months purely on GATE PYQs (Previous Year Questions).

  • Do PYQs topic-by-topic first, then full mocks in the final two months.
  • Time yourself strictly — the exam has no margin for slow solvers.
  • Mathematics and DS/Algo combined account for ~40% of marks. Prioritise these.

Picking IIT Kanpur

IITK CSE has strong research culture and excellent faculty across ML, systems, and theory. I specifically wanted to work on ML research, and IITK has multiple active groups in that space.

The first few weeks

Orientation is intense. The workload is real — assignments, quizzes, and research group meetings all compete for time. Building a solid routine early is probably the most important thing you can do.

The people around you at an IIT will push you to think harder than you thought possible. That is the real value.
