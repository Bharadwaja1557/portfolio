My Journey to IIT Kanpur MTech CSE

Getting into IIT Kanpur for MTech CSE was one of the most rewarding things I've done. It required about a year of focused preparation, a clear strategy, and some lessons learned the hard way. Here's what I wish I'd known when I started.

The GATE exam

GATE CS is the primary gateway. The syllabus is wide — Data Structures, Algorithms, OS, DBMS, Computer Networks, Theory of Computation, Discrete Math, and Linear Algebra — but the depth required at each topic is manageable if you plan well.

I spent the first three months doing a systematic subject-by-subject pass using standard textbooks (CLRS for Algorithms, Galvin for OS, Forouzan for Networks). The next four months were entirely problem-focused — GATE PYQs (Previous Year Questions) are gold.

  • Do PYQs topic-by-topic first, then full mock tests in the final two months.
  • Time yourself strictly — the real exam has no margin for slow solvers.
  • Mathematics and DS/Algo combined account for ~40% of marks. Prioritise these.

Picking IIT Kanpur

IIT Kanpur's CSE department has a strong research culture and excellent faculty across ML, systems, and theory. The peer group is outstanding, and the campus infrastructure makes it easy to stay focused. I specifically wanted to work on ML research, and IITK has multiple active groups in that space.

The first few weeks

Orientation is intense. You go from zero to a full course load very quickly. The workload is real — assignments, quizzes, and research group meetings all compete for time. Building a solid routine early is probably the most important thing you can do.

The people around you at an IIT will push you to think harder than you thought possible. That's the real value.
